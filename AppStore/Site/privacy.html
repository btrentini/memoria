Memoria

Privacy policy

Memoria processes system readings on your Mac. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking or remote telemetry. The developer does not receive system readings through the app.

Local storage

Display settings remain in the app’s private container. Chart history is held in memory for up to one hour and is cleared on quit. When you enable desktop widgets, Memoria writes a bounded aggregate CPU and memory snapshot to its shared local container. Switching widget sharing off deletes the snapshot. macOS may retain a displayed widget until it refreshes.

Support

If you choose to open a public GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and is publicly visible. Do not include process lists, personal paths, credentials or other private information. This support interaction is separate from Memoria’s local monitoring.

Changes and contact

This policy describes the App Store edition prepared on 27 October 2026. Contact the project through its support page for questions. A public hosted copy of this policy must be available before App Store submission.
